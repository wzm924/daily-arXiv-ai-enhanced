### [1] [Fractional porous medium equation on manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature: existence of solutions and smoothing effects via potential methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12954)
*Dorothea-Enrica von Criegern,Gabriele Grillo,Dario Daniele Monticelli*

Main category: math.AP

TL;DR: 研究完备非紧、Ricci非负流形上的分数阶多孔介质方程，引入Green加权初值空间并建立弱对偶（势）解的存在性与（局部/全局）光滑性估计，证明其在短时与长时意义下最优，并推广到更一般的渗流方程。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 分数阶Laplace算子在非紧流形上缺乏显式核与平移不变性，经典的$L^1$初值框架难以直接刻画解的适定性与正则性；且在欧氏空间已有反例表明$L^1$外自然加权数据未必产生有界解。作者希望利用极小的Green函数构造合适的加权初值空间，从而在几何（Ricci曲率、非坍塌、体积增长）条件下统一处理存在性与光滑性，并澄清最优性。

Method: 在$s\in(0,1]$、$n>2s$、$\mathrm{Ric}\ge0$的完备非紧流形上，假设流形$s$-非抛物以得到分数阶Laplacian的极小正Green函数$G$；以$G$为权定义比$L^1$更大的加权初值空间，并据此给出方程的对偶/势形式的弱解框架，证明非负初值的存在性；随后对$L^1$与Green加权数据分别建立定量局部光滑估计，在额外非坍塌与一致体积增长假设下推出全局光滑估计；并构造反例（包括$\mathbb{R}^n$情形）说明需附加一致加权可积性才能保证有界解，最后将该方法推广至更一般的渗流型方程。

Result: 证明了Green加权空间中非负初值弱（势）解的存在性；得到$L^1$与Green加权数据下的定量局部光滑估计，并在非坍塌与一致体积增长条件下获得全局光滑估计；证明估计所预测的短时与长时行为在相应意义下最优；指出在格林加权空间中数据即使位于$\mathbb{R}^n$也不必产生有界解，除非附加合适的统一加权可积性条件；对$s\in(0,1/2)$仅需$\mathrm{Ric}\ge0$及必要的非坍塌假设；结果覆盖$s=1$并给出若干新结论，同时推广到更一般的渗流方程。

Conclusion: 该工作以极小Green函数为核心工具，在非负Ricci曲率流形上为分数阶多孔介质方程建立了统一的存在性与光滑性理论，将初值框架由$L^1$扩展到Green加权空间，并刻画了光滑估计的最优性；方法具有可推广性，适用于$s=1$及更一般的渗流方程。

Abstract: We study the fractional porous medium equation on complete noncompact Riemannian manifolds with nonnegative Ricci curvature for $s\in(0,1]$ and $n>2s$. Assuming that the manifold is $s$-nonparabolic, so that the fractional Laplacian admits a suitable positive minimal Green function, we use this Green function to introduce a natural weighted space of initial data, strictly larger than $L^1$. This leads to a weak dual, or potential, formulation of the equation, for which we prove existence for nonnegative initial data in the weighted space. We then establish quantitative local smoothing estimates for initial data in either $L^1$ or the Green-weighted space. Under additional noncollapsing and uniform volume-growth assumptions, we obtain global smoothing estimates. We also show that, even in $\mathbb{R}^n$, data in the Green-weighted space need not generate bounded solutions unless a suitable uniform weighted integrability condition is imposed. The short- and long-time behaviors predicted by our estimates are shown to be optimal in appropriate senses. When $s\in(0,1/2)$, the results require only $\operatorname{Ric}\geq0$, together with a noncollapsing assumption where needed. Our estimates also cover the case $s=1$, several of them being new in that setting. Finally, we extend the approach to more general filtration equations.

Assuming that the diffusivity and the sound speed are known a priori, we investigate the simultaneous stable determination of the friction coefficient, the weak damping coefficient, the potential, and the nonlinear coefficient from the associated Dirichlet-to-Neumann map. These arguments reduce the inverse problem to stability estimates for (attenuated) geodesic ray transforms under the foliation condition. As a consequence, we obtain Hölder-type stability estimates of recovering the linear and nonlinear coefficients.
